From poor to very good composition

What Lichi shows

  • A score from 0 to 10, based on nutrition and ingredients, not just calories.
  • Positive and negative: what raised or lowered the score — sugar, salt, saturated fat, fibre, protein, fruit and vegetables, additives.
  • Ingredients with colour marks and short notes.
  • Allergens, when the ingredient list names them.
  • Nutrition per 100 g or 100 ml.

Ukrainian products

Our own database of over 70,000 products from Ukrainian shops, with photos. If a product is not there, Lichi looks it up in the open Open Food Facts database.

Good to know

Lichi’s score describes the product itself, not your health. It is not medical advice. If you have allergies or a special diet, always check the pack.
